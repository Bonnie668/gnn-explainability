Abstract摘要

EN · English original 英文原文
中文 · Chinese translation 中文翻译

Despite recent progress in Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), explaining predictions made by GNNs remains a challenging open problem. The leading method independently addresses the local explanations (i.e., important subgraph structure and node features) to interpret why a GNN model makes the prediction for a single instance, e.g. a node or a graph. As a result, the explanation generated is painstakingly customized for each instance. The unique explanation interpreting each instance independently is not sufficient to provide a global understanding of the learned GNN model, leading to the lack of generalizability and hindering it from being used in the inductive setting. Besides, as it is designed for explaining a single instance, it is challenging to explain a set of instances naturally (e.g., graphs of a given class). In this study, we address these key challenges and propose PGExplainer, a parameterized explainer for GNNs. PGExplainer adopts a deep neural network to parameterize the generation process of explanations, which enables PGExplainer a natural approach to explaining multiple instances collectively. Compared to the existing work, PGExplainer has better generalization ability and can be utilized in an inductive setting easily. Experiments on both synthetic and real-life datasets show highly competitive performance with up to 24.7% relative improvement in AUC on explaining graph classification over the leading baseline.

尽管图神经网络（GNN）近期取得了长足进展，解释 GNN 所做出的预测仍然是一个极具挑战的开放问题。目前的主流方法独立地求解局部解释（即重要的子图结构与节点特征），以说明 GNN 模型为何对单个实例（例如一个节点或一张图）做出预测。因此，它生成的解释是为每个实例逐一精心定制的。这种对每个实例独立求解的单一解释，不足以提供对所学 GNN 模型的全局理解，导致其缺乏泛化能力，也难以应用于归纳式（inductive）场景。此外，由于该方法是为解释单个实例而设计的，要自然地解释一组实例（例如某一类别的图）十分困难。在本研究中，我们针对这些关键挑战提出了 PGExplainer——一种面向 GNN 的参数化解释器。PGExplainer 采用深度神经网络对解释的生成过程进行参数化，从而能够以集体（collective）方式自然地解释多个实例。与现有工作相比，PGExplainer 具有更好的泛化能力，并且可以轻松用于归纳式场景。在合成数据集与真实数据集上的实验表明，在图分类解释任务上，PGExplainer 相比最优基线在 AUC 上实现了最高 24.7% 的相对提升，性能极具竞争力。

Introduction引言

Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) are powerful tools for representation learning of graph-structured data, such as social networks [46], document citation graphs [33], and microbiological graphs [44]. GNNs broadly adopt a message passing scheme to learn node representations by aggregating representation vectors of its neighbors [49, 16]. This scheme enables GNN to capture both node features and graph topology. GNN-based methods have achieved state-of-the-art performance in node classification, graph classification, and link prediction, etc [21, 45, 56].

图神经网络（GNN）是图结构数据表示学习的强大工具，例如社交网络 [46]、文献引用图 [33] 和微生物图 [44]。GNN 广泛采用消息传递机制，通过聚合邻居的表示向量来学习节点表示 [49, 16]。这种机制使 GNN 能够同时捕捉节点特征和图拓扑结构。基于 GNN 的方法已在节点分类、图分类和链路预测等任务上达到最先进的性能水平 [21, 45, 56]。

Despite their remarkable effectiveness, the rationales of predictions made by GNNs are not easy for humans to understand. Since GNNs aggregate both node features and graph topology to make predictions, to understand predictions made by GNNs, important subgraphs and/or a set of features, which are also known as explanations, need to be uncovered. In the literature, although a variety of efforts have been undertaken to interpret general deep neural networks, existing approaches [6, 26, 12, 31, 43, 19, 20] in this line fall short in their ability to explain graph structures, which is essential for GNNs. Explaining predictions made by GNNs remains a challenging open problem, on which few methods have been proposed. The combinatorial nature of explaining graph structures makes it difficult to design models that are both robust and efficient.

尽管 GNN 效果显著，但其预测背后的理据（rationale）对人类而言并不容易理解。由于 GNN 是聚合节点特征与图拓扑来做出预测的，要理解 GNN 的预测，就需要找出重要的子图和/或一组特征，这些也被称为解释。文献中虽然已有大量工作致力于解释通用深度神经网络，但这一方向的现有方法 [6, 26, 12, 31, 43, 19, 20] 在解释图结构方面能力不足，而图结构解释对 GNN 至关重要。解释 GNN 的预测仍是一个具有挑战性的开放问题，迄今提出的方法寥寥无几。解释图结构的组合特性使得设计既稳健又高效的模型十分困难。

Recently, the first general model-agnostic approach for GNNs, GNNExplainer [53], was proposed to address the problem. It takes a trained GNN and its predictions as inputs to provide interpretable explanations for a given instance, e.g. a node or a graph. The explanation includes a compact subgraph structure and a small subset of node features that are crucial in GNN’s prediction for the target instance. Nevertheless, there are several limitations in the existing approach. First, GNNExplainer largely focuses on providing the local interpretability by generating a painstakingly customized explanation for a single instance individually and independently. The explanation provided by GNNExplainer is limited to the single instance, making GNNExplainer difficult to be applied in the inductive setting because the explanations are hard to generalize to other unexplained nodes. As pointed out in previous studies, models interpreting each instance independently are not sufficient to provide a global understanding of the trained model [19]. Furthermore, GNNExplainer has to be retrained for every single explanation. As a result, in real-life scenarios where plenty of nodes need to be interpreted, GNNExplainer would be time-consuming and impractical. Moreover, as GNNExplainer was developed for interpreting individual instances, the explanatory motifs are not learned end-to-end with a global view of the whole GNN model. Thus, it may suffer from suboptimal generalization performance. How to explain predictions of GNNs on a set of instances collectively and easily generalize the learned explainer model to other instances in the inductive setting remains largely unexplored in the literature.

最近，针对该问题提出了首个通用的模型无关方法 GNNExplainer [53]。它以训练好的 GNN 及其预测为输入，为给定实例（例如一个节点或一张图）提供可解释的解释，解释包含对目标实例预测至关重要的紧凑子图结构以及一小部分节点特征。然而，现有方法存在若干局限。首先，GNNExplainer 主要通过为单个实例逐一独立地生成精心定制的解释来提供局部可解释性，其解释局限于单一实例，难以应用于归纳式场景，因为这些解释很难泛化到其他未被解释的节点。正如先前研究指出的，独立解释每个实例的模型不足以提供对训练模型的全局理解 [19]。此外，GNNExplainer 必须为每一条解释重新训练模型。因此，在需要解释大量节点的真实场景中，GNNExplainer 会耗费大量时间，并不实用。再者，由于 GNNExplainer 是为解释单个实例而开发的，解释性基元（motif）并非以整个 GNN 模型的全局视角进行端到端学习，因此其泛化性能可能次优。如何在集合上集体解释 GNN 的预测、并在归纳式场景中轻松地将学得的解释器模型泛化到其他实例，在文献中仍基本未被探索。

To provide a global understanding of predictions made by GNNs, in this study, we emphasize the collective and inductive nature of this problem and present our method PGExplainer (Figure 1). PGExplainer is a general explainer that applies to any GNN based models in both transductive and inductive settings. Specifically, a generative probabilistic model for graph data is utilized in PGExplainer. Generative models have shown the power to learn succinct underlying structures from the observed graph data [23]. PGExplainer uncovers these underlying structures as the explanations, which is believed to make the most contribution to GNNs’ predictions [35]. We model the underlying structure as edge distributions, where the explanatory graph is sampled. To collectively explain predictions of multiple instances, the generation process in PGExplainer is parameterized with a deep neural network. Since the neural network parameters are shared across the population of explained instances, PGExplainer is naturally applicable to provide model-level explanations for each instance with a global view of the GNN model. Furthermore, PGExplainer has better generalization power because a trained PGExplainer model can be utilized in an inductive setting to infer explanations of unexplained nodes without retraining the explanation model. This also makes PGExplainer much faster than the existing approaches.

为了提供对 GNN 预测的全局理解，在本研究中我们强调该问题的集体性与归纳性，并提出我们的方法 PGExplainer（图 1）。PGExplainer 是一种通用解释器，适用于任何基于 GNN 的模型，既可工作于直推式（transductive）也可工作于归纳式场景。具体而言，PGExplainer 利用了一个面向图数据的生成式概率模型。生成模型已被证明能够从观测到的图数据中学习简洁的底层结构 [23]。PGExplainer 将这些底层结构揭示为解释，而这些结构被认为对 GNN 的预测贡献最大 [35]。我们将底层结构建模为边分布（edge distributions），解释图从中采样得到。为了集体解释多个实例的预测，PGExplainer 中的生成过程由一个深度神经网络参数化。由于神经网络参数在被解释实例群体间共享，PGExplainer 自然能够以 GNN 模型的全局视角为每个实例提供模型级解释。此外，PGExplainer 具有更强的泛化能力，因为训练好的 PGExplainer 模型可以在归纳式场景中推断未被解释节点的解释，而无需重新训练解释模型。这也使得 PGExplainer 比现有方法快得多。

Experimental results on both synthetic and real-life datasets demonstrate that PGExplainer can achieve consistent and accurate explanations, bringing up to 24.7% improvement in AUC over the SOTA method on explaining graph classification with significant speed-up.

在合成数据集和真实数据集上的实验结果表明，PGExplainer 能够生成一致且准确的解释，在解释图分类任务上相比 SOTA 方法 AUC 最高提升 24.7%，并带来显著的加速。

Related Work相关工作

2.1Graph neural networks图神经网络

Graph Neural networks (GNNs) have achieved remarkable success in various tasks, including node classification [25, 21, 45, 48], graph classification [10], and link prediction [56]. The study of GNNs was initiated in [17], and then extended in [41]. These methods iteratively aggregate neighbor information to learn node representations until reaching a static state. Inspired by the success of convolutional neural networks (CNNs) in computer vision, attempts of applying convolutional operations to graphs were derived based on graph spectral theory [4] and graph Fourier transformation [42]. In recent work, GNNs broadly encode node features as messages and adopt the message passing mechanism to propagate and aggregate them along edges to learn node/graph representations, which are then utilized for downstream tasks [25, 10, 41, 29, 45, 34, 47]. For efficiency consideration, localized filters were proposed to reduce computation cost [21]. The self-attention mechanism was introduced to GNNs in GAT to differentiate the importance of neighbors [45]. Xu. et al. analyzed the relationship between GNNs and Weisfeiler-Lehman graph isomorphism test, and showed the express power of GNNs [49].

图神经网络（GNN）在各种任务中取得了显著成功，包括节点分类 [25, 21, 45, 48]、图分类 [10] 和链路预测 [56]。GNN 的研究始于 [17]，并在 [41] 中得到扩展。这些方法迭代地聚合邻居信息以学习节点表示，直至达到静态状态。受卷积神经网络（CNN）在计算机视觉领域成功的启发，基于图谱理论 [4] 和图傅里叶变换 [42] 的图卷积操作尝试相继出现。在近期工作中，GNN 广泛地将节点特征编码为消息，并采用消息传递机制沿边传播与聚合这些消息以学习节点/图表示，再用于下游任务 [25, 10, 41, 29, 45, 34, 47]。出于效率考虑，人们提出了局部滤波器以降低计算成本 [21]。GAT 将自注意力机制引入 GNN，以区分邻居的重要性 [45]。Xu 等人分析了 GNN 与 Weisfeiler-Lehman 图同构测试之间的关系，展示了 GNN 的表达能力 [49]。

2.2Explaining GNNs解释 GNN

Interpretability and feature selection have been extensively addressed in neural networks. Methods demystifying complicated deep learning models can be grouped into two main families, whitebox and blackbox [19, 20]. Whitebox mechanisms mainly focus on yielding explanations for individual predictions. Forward and backward propagation based methods are used routinely in whitebox mechanisms. Forward propagation based methods broadly perturb the input and/or hidden representations and check the corresponding updating results in the downstream task [8, 14, 28]. The underlying intuition is that the outputs of the downstream task are likely to significantly change if important features are occluded. Backward propagation based methods, in general, infer important features from the gradients of the deep neural networks. They compute weights of features by propagating the gradients from the output back to the input. Blackbox methods generate explanations by locally learning interpretable models, such as linear models, and additive models to approximate the predictions [32, 5].

可解释性与特征选择在神经网络中已被广泛研究。揭开复杂深度学习模型面纱的方法主要分为两个大类：白盒（whitebox）与黑盒（blackbox）[19, 20]。白盒机制主要侧重于为单个预测生成解释，其中常用基于前向传播与后向传播的方法。基于前向传播的方法通常扰动输入和/或隐藏表示，并检查下游任务中相应的更新结果 [8, 14, 28]，其直觉是：如果重要特征被遮挡，下游任务的输出很可能发生显著变化。基于后向传播的方法通常从深度神经网络的梯度中推断重要特征，通过将梯度从输出反向传播到输入来计算特征的权重。黑盒方法则通过局部学习可解释模型（如线性模型、加性模型）来近似预测，从而生成解释 [32, 5]。

Following the line of forward propagation methods, GNNExplainer initiates the research on explaining predictions on graph-structured data [53]. It excludes certain edges and node features to observe the changes in node/graph classification. Explanations (subgraphs/important features) are extracted by maximizing the mutual information between the distribution of possible subgraphs and the GNN’s prediction. However, similar to other forward propagation methods, GNNExplainer generates customized explanations for single instance prediction independently, making it insufficient to provide a global understanding of the trained GNN model. Besides, it is naturally difficult to be applied in the inductive setting and provide explanations for multiple instances. XGNN provides model-level explanations without preserving the local fidelity [55]. Thus, the generated explanation may not be a substructure of the real input graph. On the other hand, PGExplainer can provide an explanation for each instance with a global view of the GNN model, which can preserve the local fidelity.

沿前向传播方法的路线，GNNExplainer 开创了图结构数据上预测解释的研究 [53]。它排除某些边和节点特征，观察节点/图分类的变化，并通过最大化可能子图的分布与 GNN 预测之间的互信息来提取解释（子图/重要特征）。然而，与其他前向传播方法类似，GNNExplainer 独立地为单个实例的预测生成定制化解释，不足以提供对训练 GNN 模型的全局理解；此外，它天然难以应用于归纳式场景并为多个实例提供解释。XGNN 提供模型级解释，但不保留局部保真度 [55]，因此生成的解释可能不是真实输入图的子结构。相比之下，PGExplainer 能够以 GNN 模型的全局视角为每个实例提供解释，从而保留局部保真度。

2.3Graph generation图生成

PGExplainer learns a probabilistic graph generative model to provide explanations for GNNs. The first model generating random graph is the Erdős–Rényi model [15, 11]. In the random graph proposed by Gilbert, each potential edge is independently chosen from a Bernoulli distribution. Some approaches generate graphs with certain properties reflected, such as pairwise distances betweenness [7], node degree distribution [27], and spectral properties [22, 2]. In recent years, deep learning models have shown great potential to generate graphs with complex properties preserved [54, 18, 35]. However, these methods mainly aim to generate graphs that reflect certain properties in the training graphs.

PGExplainer 学习一个概率图生成模型以为 GNN 提供解释。生成随机图的第一个模型是 Erdős–Rényi 模型 [15, 11]。在 Gilbert 提出的随机图中，每条潜在边独立地服从伯努利分布。一些方法生成反映特定属性的图，例如点对距离介数 [7]、节点度分布 [27] 和谱属性 [22, 2]。近年来，深度学习模型在生成保留复杂属性的图方面展现出巨大潜力 [54, 18, 35]。然而，这些方法主要旨在生成反映训练图中某些属性的图。

Background背景

3.1Notations记号

We first describe notations, and then provide some background on graph neural networks. Let $G = (V, E)$ represent the graph with $V = \{v_1, v_2 \ldots v_N\}$ denoting the node set and $E \in V \times V$ as the edge set. The numbers of nodes and edges are denoted by $N$ and $M$, respectively. A graph can be described by an adjacency matrix $A \in \{0,1\}^{N \times N}$, with $a_{ij} = 1$ if there is an edge connecting node $i$ and $j$, and $a_{ij} = 0$ otherwise. Nodes in $V$ are associated with the $d$-dimensional features, denoted by $X \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d}$.

我们首先描述记号，然后提供一些图神经网络的背景知识。设 $G = (V, E)$ 表示图，其中 $V = \{v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_N\}$ 表示节点集，$E \in V \times V$ 表示边集。节点数和边数分别记为 $N$ 和 $M$。图可以用邻接矩阵 $A \in \{0,1\}^{N \times N}$ 描述：若存在连接节点 $i$ 和 $j$ 的边则 $a_{ij} = 1$，否则 $a_{ij} = 0$。$V$ 中的节点关联 $d$ 维特征，记为 $X \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times d}$。

3.2Graph neural networks图神经网络

GNNs adopt the message-passing mechanism to propagate and aggregate information along edges in the input graph to learn node representations [25, 21, 45, 16]. Each GNN layer includes three essential steps. First, at the propagation step of the $i$-th GNN layer, for each edge $(i, j)$, GNN computes a message $m^l_{ij} = \text{Message}(h^{l-1}_i, h^{l-1}_j)$, where $h^{l-1}_i$ and $h^{l-1}_j$ are representations of $v_i$ and $v_j$ in previous layer, respectively. Second, at the aggregation step, for each node $v_i$, GNN aggregates messages received from its neighbor nodes, denoted by $\mathcal{N}_i$, with an aggregation function $m^l_{i\cdot} = \text{aggregation}(\{m^l_{ij} \mid j \in \mathcal{N}_i\})$. Last, at the updating step, GNN updates the vector representation for each node $v_i$ via $h^l_i = \text{update}(m^l_{i\cdot}, h^{l-1}_i)$, a function taking the aggregated message and the representation of itself as inputs. Hidden representation of the last GNN layer serves as the final node representation: $z_i = h^L_i$, which is then used for downstream tasks, such as node/graph classification and link prediction.

GNN 采用消息传递机制沿输入图的边传播与聚合信息，以学习节点表示 [25, 21, 45, 16]。每个 GNN 层包含三个基本步骤。首先，在第 $i$ 个 GNN 层的传播步骤中，对每条边 $(i, j)$，GNN 计算消息 $m^l_{ij} = \text{Message}(h^{l-1}_i, h^{l-1}_j)$，其中 $h^{l-1}_i$ 和 $h^{l-1}_j$ 分别是 $v_i$ 和 $v_j$ 在上一层的表示。其次，在聚合步骤中，对每个节点 $v_i$，GNN 使用聚合函数 $m^l_{i\cdot} = \text{aggregation}(\{m^l_{ij} \mid j \in \mathcal{N}_i\})$ 聚合从其邻居节点（记为 $\mathcal{N}_i$）接收到的消息。最后，在更新步骤中，GNN 通过 $h^l_i = \text{update}(m^l_{i\cdot}, h^{l-1}_i)$ 更新每个节点 $v_i$ 的向量表示，该函数以聚合消息和节点自身表示作为输入。最后一层 GNN 层的隐藏表示作为最终节点表示：$z_i = h^L_i$，随后用于下游任务，如节点/图分类和链路预测。

The PGExplainerPGExplainer 方法

In this section, we introduce PGExplainer. Different from GNNExplainer which provides explanations on both structure and features, PGExplainer focuses on explanation on graph structures because feature explanation in GNNs is similar to that in non-graph neural networks, which has been extensively studied in the literature [1, 19, 32, 39, 8, 14, 28]. PGExplainer is flexible and applicable to interpret all kinds of GNNs. We start with the learning objective of PGExplainer (Section 4.1) and then present the reparameterization strategy for efficient optimization (Section 4.2). In Section 4.3, we specify particular instantiations to understand GNNs on node and graph classifications. Detailed algorithms can be found in the Appendix.

在本节中，我们介绍 PGExplainer。与同时提供结构和特征解释的 GNNExplainer 不同，PGExplainer 专注于图结构解释，因为 GNN 中的特征解释与非图神经网络中的特征解释类似，而后者在文献中已被广泛研究 [1, 19, 32, 39, 8, 14, 28]。PGExplainer 灵活且适用于解释各类 GNN。我们先介绍 PGExplainer 的学习目标（第 4.1 节），然后给出用于高效优化的重参数化策略（第 4.2 节）。在第 4.3 节中，我们给出在节点分类和图分类任务上理解 GNN 的具体实例化。详细算法见附录。

4.1The learning objective学习目标

The literature has shown that real-life graphs are with underling structures [35, 37]. To explain predictions made by a GNN model, we divide the original input graph $G_o$ into two subgraphs: $G_o = G_s + \Delta G$, where $G_s$ presents the underlying subgraph that makes important contributions to GNN’s predictions, which is the expected explanatory graph, and $\Delta G$ consists of the remaining task-irrelevant edges for predictions made by the GNN. Following [53], PGExplainer finds $G_s$ by maximizing the mutual information between the GNN’s predictions and the underlying structure $G_s$:

文献表明真实世界的图具有底层结构 [35, 37]。为了解释 GNN 模型做出的预测，我们将原始输入图 $G_o$ 划分为两个子图：$G_o = G_s + \Delta G$，其中 $G_s$ 表示对 GNN 预测有重要贡献的底层子图，即期望得到的解释图；$\Delta G$ 由其余与 GNN 预测任务无关的边组成。参照 [53]，PGExplainer 通过最大化 GNN 预测与底层结构 $G_s$ 之间的互信息来求解 $G_s$：

$$\max_{G_s} \; \text{MI}(Y_o, G_s) = H(Y_o) - H(Y_o \mid G = G_s) \tag{1}$$

where $Y_o$ is the prediction of the GNN model with $G_o$ as the input. The mutual information quantifies the probability of prediction $Y_o$ when the input graph to the GNN model is limited to the explanatory graph $G_s$. The intuition behind comes from the traditional forward propagation based methods for the whitebox explanation [8]. For example, if removing an edge $(i, j)$ dramatically changes the prediction in the GNN, then this edge is important and should be included in $G_s$. Otherwise, it can be considered as irrelevant edge for the GNN model to make the prediction. Since $H(Y_o)$ is only related to the GNN model whose parameters are fixed in the explanation stage, the objective is equivalent to minimizing the conditional entropy $H(Y_o \mid G = G_s)$.

其中 $Y_o$ 是以 $G_o$ 为输入时 GNN 模型的预测。互信息量化了当输入到 GNN 模型的图被限制为解释图 $G_s$ 时预测 $Y_o$ 出现的概率。其直觉来自传统的白盒解释前向传播方法 [8]。例如，如果移除边 $(i, j)$ 会显著改变 GNN 的预测，那么这条边很重要，应包含在 $G_s$ 中；否则，可以认为它是 GNN 模型做出预测时的非相关边。由于 $H(Y_o)$ 仅与 GNN 模型有关，而模型参数在解释阶段是固定的，因此该目标等价于最小化条件熵 $H(Y_o \mid G = G_s)$。

However, the direct optimization of the above objective function is intractable as there are $2^M$ candidates for $G_s$. Thus, we consider a relaxation by assuming that the explanatory graph is a Gilbert random graph [15], where selections of edges from the original input graph $G_o$ are conditionally independent to each other. Let $e_{ij} \in V \times V$ be the binary variable indicating whether the edge is selected, with $e_{ij} = 1$ if the edge $(i, j)$ is selected, and 0 otherwise. Let $G$ be the random graph variable. Based on the above assumption, the probability of a graph $G$ can be factorized as:

然而，直接优化上述目标函数是不可行的，因为 $G_s$ 有 $2^M$ 种候选。因此，我们考虑一种松弛：假设解释图是一个 Gilbert 随机图 [15]，其中对原始输入图 $G_o$ 中各边的选择彼此条件独立。设 $e_{ij} \in V \times V$ 为指示边是否被选中的二元变量：若边 $(i, j)$ 被选中则 $e_{ij} = 1$，否则为 0。设 $G$ 为随机图变量。基于上述假设，图 $G$ 的概率可分解为：

$$P(G) = \prod_{(i,j) \in E} P(e_{ij}) \tag{2}$$

A straightforward instantiation of $P(e_{ij})$ is the Bernoulli distribution $e_{ij} \sim \text{Bern}(\theta_{ij})$. $P(e_{ij} = 1) = \theta_{ij}$ is the probability that edge $(i, j)$ exists in $G$. With this relaxation, we thus can rewrite the objective as:

$P(e_{ij})$ 的一个直接实例化是伯努利分布 $e_{ij} \sim \text{Bern}(\theta_{ij})$，$P(e_{ij} = 1) = \theta_{ij}$ 是边 $(i, j)$ 存在于 $G$ 中的概率。通过这种松弛，我们可以将目标改写为：

$$\min_{G_s} H(Y_o \mid G = G_s) = \min_{G_s} \mathbb{E}_{G_s}\left[H(Y_o \mid G = G_s)\right] \approx \min_{\Theta} \; \mathbb{E}_{G_s \sim q(\Theta)}\left[H(Y_o \mid G = G_s)\right] \tag{3}$$

where $q(\Theta)$ is the distribution of the explanatory graph parameterized by $\theta$’s.

其中 $q(\Theta)$ 是以 $\theta$ 为参数的解释图分布。

4.2The reparameterization trick重参数化技巧

Due to the discrete nature of $G_s$, we relax edge weights from binary variables to continuous variables in the range $(0, 1)$ and adopt the reparameterization trick to efficiently optimize the objective function with gradient-based methods [24]. We approximate the sampling process $G_s \sim q(\Theta)$ with a determinant function of parameters $\Omega$, temperature $\tau$, and an independent random variable $\varepsilon$: $G_s \approx \hat{G}_s = f_\Omega(G_o, \tau, \varepsilon)$. The temperature $\tau$ is used to control the approximation. Here we utilize the binary concrete distribution as the instantiation [36]. Specifically, the weight $\hat{e}_{ij} \in (0, 1)$ of edge $(i, j)$ in $\hat{G}_s$ is calculated by:

由于 $G_s$ 的离散性质，我们将边权从二元变量松弛为 $(0, 1)$ 范围内的连续变量，并采用重参数化技巧，以基于梯度的优化方法高效优化目标函数 [24]。我们用参数 $\Omega$、温度 $\tau$ 以及独立随机变量 $\varepsilon$ 的确定性函数来近似采样过程 $G_s \sim q(\Theta)$：$G_s \approx \hat{G}_s = f_\Omega(G_o, \tau, \varepsilon)$。温度 $\tau$ 用于控制近似的程度。这里我们采用二元 concrete 分布作为实例化 [36]。具体而言，$\hat{G}_s$ 中边 $(i, j)$ 的权重 $\hat{e}_{ij} \in (0, 1)$ 计算如下：

$$\varepsilon \sim \text{Uniform}(0, 1), \qquad \hat{e}_{ij} = \sigma\!\left(\frac{\log \varepsilon - \log(1 - \varepsilon) + \omega_{ij}}{\tau}\right) \tag{4}$$

where $\sigma(\cdot)$ is the Sigmoid function, and $\omega_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}$ is the parameter. When $\tau \to 0$, the weight $\hat{e}_{ij}$ is binarized with $\lim_{\tau \to 0} P(\hat{e}_{ij} = 1) = \frac{\exp(\omega_{ij})}{1 + \exp(\omega_{ij})}$. Since $P(e_{ij} = 1) = \theta_{ij}$, by choosing $\omega_{ij} = \log \frac{\theta_{ij}}{1 - \theta_{ij}}$, we have $\lim_{\tau \to 0} \hat{G}_s = G_s$. This demonstrates the rationality of using binary concrete distribution to approximate the Bernoulli distribution. Moreover, with temperature $\tau > 0$, the objective function is smoothed with a well-defined gradient $\frac{\partial \hat{e}_{ij}}{\partial \omega_{ij}}$. Thus, with reparameterization, the objective in Eq. (3) becomes:

其中 $\sigma(\cdot)$ 为 Sigmoid 函数，$\omega_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}$ 为参数。当 $\tau \to 0$ 时，权重 $\hat{e}_{ij}$ 被二值化，$\lim_{\tau \to 0} P(\hat{e}_{ij} = 1) = \frac{\exp(\omega_{ij})}{1 + \exp(\omega_{ij})}$。由于 $P(e_{ij} = 1) = \theta_{ij}$，通过选取 $\omega_{ij} = \log \frac{\theta_{ij}}{1 - \theta_{ij}}$，我们有 $\lim_{\tau \to 0} \hat{G}_s = G_s$。这证明了用二元 concrete 分布近似伯努利分布的合理性。此外，当温度 $\tau > 0$ 时，目标函数被平滑化，梯度 $\frac{\partial \hat{e}_{ij}}{\partial \omega_{ij}}$ 良定义。因此，通过重参数化，式 (3) 中的目标变为：

$$\min_{\Omega} \; \mathbb{E}_{\varepsilon \sim \text{Uniform}(0,1)} \; H(Y_o \mid G = \hat{G}_s) \tag{5}$$

Considering efficient optimization, we follow [53] to modify the conditional entropy with cross-entropy $H(Y_o, \hat{Y}_s)$, where $\hat{Y}_s$ is the prediction of the GNN model with $\hat{G}_s$ as the input. With the above relaxations, we adopt Monte Carlo to approximately optimize the objective function:

考虑到高效优化，我们参照 [53] 用交叉熵 $H(Y_o, \hat{Y}_s)$ 修正条件熵，其中 $\hat{Y}_s$ 是以 $\hat{G}_s$ 为输入时 GNN 模型的预测。基于上述松弛，我们采用蒙特卡洛方法近似优化目标函数：

$$\min_{\Omega} \; \mathbb{E}_{\varepsilon \sim \text{Uniform}(0,1)} \; H(Y_o, \hat{Y}_s) \approx \min_{\Omega} \; -\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \sum_{c=1}^{C} P(Y_o = c) \log P(\hat{Y}_s = c) = \min_{\Omega} \; -\frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \sum_{c=1}^{C} P_\Phi(Y = c \mid G = G_o) \log P_\Phi(Y = c \mid G = \hat{G}_s^{(k)}) \tag{6}$$

where $\Phi$ denotes the parameters in the trained GNN, $K$ is the total number of sampled graph, $C$ is the number of labels, and $\hat{G}_s^{(k)}$ is the $k$-th sampled graph with Eq. (4), parameterized by $\Omega$.

其中 $\Phi$ 表示训练好的 GNN 中的参数，$K$ 是采样图的总数，$C$ 是标签数量，$\hat{G}_s^{(k)}$ 是由式 (4) 参数化（参数为 $\Omega$）的第 $k$ 个采样图。

4.3Explanation of graph neural networks with a global view以全局视角解释图神经网络

Although explanations provided by the leading method GNNExplainer [53] preserve the local fidelity, they do not help to understand the general picture of the model across a population [50]. Furthermore, various GNN based models have been applied to analyze graph data with millions of instances [52], the cost of applying local explanations one-by-one can be prohibitive with such large datasets in practice. On the other hand, explanations with a global view of the model ascertain users’ trust [39]. Furthermore, these models can generalize explanations to new instances without retraining, making it more efficient to explain large scale datasets.

尽管主流方法 GNNExplainer [53] 提供的解释保留了局部保真度，但它们无助于理解模型在群体上的整体图景 [50]。此外，各种基于 GNN 的模型已被应用于分析包含数百万实例的图数据 [52]，在这种大规模数据集上逐一应用局部解释的成本在实践中可能高得令人望而却步。另一方面，具有模型全局视角的解释能够确立用户的信任 [39]。此外，这些模型可以在不重新训练的情况下将解释泛化到新实例，从而更高效地解释大规模数据集。

To have a global view of a GNN model, our method collectively explains predictions made by a trained model on multiple instances. Instead of treating $\Omega$ in Eq. (6) as independent variables, we utilize a parameterized network to learn to generate explanations from the trained GNN model, which also applies to unexplained instances. In general, GNN based models first learn node representations and then feed the vector representations to downstream tasks [21, 25, 45]. We denote these two functions by $\text{GNN}_E^{\Phi_0}(\cdot)$ and $\text{GNN}_C^{\Phi_1}(\cdot)$, respectively. For GNNs without explicit classification layers, we use the last layer instead. As a result, we can represent a GNN model with:

为了获得 GNN 模型的全局视角，我们的方法集体解释训练模型对多个实例的预测。我们不是将式 (6) 中的 $\Omega$ 视为独立变量，而是利用一个参数化网络从训练好的 GNN 模型学习生成解释，该网络同样适用于未被解释的实例。一般而言，基于 GNN 的模型首先学习节点表示，然后将向量表示输入下游任务 [21, 25, 45]。我们将这两个函数分别记为 $\text{GNN}_E^{\Phi_0}(\cdot)$ 和 $\text{GNN}_C^{\Phi_1}(\cdot)$。对于没有显式分类层的 GNN，我们改用最后一层。因此，我们可以将 GNN 模型表示为：

$$Z = \text{GNN}_E^{\Phi_0}(G_o, X), \qquad Y = \text{GNN}_C^{\Phi_1}(Z) \tag{7}$$

$Z$ is the matrix of node representations encoding both features and structure of the input graph, which is used as an input in the explanation network to calculate the parameter $\Omega$:

$Z$ 是节点表示矩阵，编码了输入图的特征与结构，用作解释网络的输入以计算参数 $\Omega$：

$$\Omega = g_\Psi(G_o, Z) \tag{8}$$

$\Psi$ denotes parameters in the explanation network, which is shared by all edges among the population. Therefore, PGExplainer can be utilized to collectively provide explanations for multiple instances. Specifically, in the collective setting with instance set $I$, the objective of PGExplainer is:

$\Psi$ 表示解释网络中的参数，在群体中的所有边之间共享。因此，PGExplainer 可以集体地为多个实例提供解释。具体而言，在实例集为 $I$ 的集体设置中，PGExplainer 的目标为：

$$\min_{\Psi} \; -\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{k=1}^{K} \sum_{c=1}^{C} P_\Phi(Y = c \mid G = G_o^{(i)}) \log P_\Phi(Y = c \mid G = \hat{G}_s^{(i,k)}) \tag{9}$$

where $G_o^{(i)}$ is the input graph and $\hat{G}_s^{(i,k)}$ is the $k$-th sampled graph with Eq. (4, 8) for $i$-th instance. We consider both graph and node classifications and specify an instantiation for each task. Solutions for other tasks, such as link prediction, are similar and thus omitted.

其中 $G_o^{(i)}$ 是输入图，$\hat{G}_s^{(i,k)}$ 是对第 $i$ 个实例由式 (4, 8) 得到的第 $k$ 个采样图。我们同时考虑图分类与节点分类，并为每个任务给出一个实例化。其他任务（如链路预测）的解法类似，故略去。

Explanation network for node classification. Considering that explanations for nodes in a graph may appear diverse structures [53], especially for nodes with different labels. For example, an edge $(i, j)$ is important for the prediction of node $u$, but not for another node $v$. Based on this motivation, we implement the $\Omega = g_\Psi(G_o, Z)$ to explain the prediction of node $v$ with:

节点分类的解释网络。考虑到图中节点的解释可能呈现不同结构 [53]，尤其是标签不同的节点。例如，边 $(i, j)$ 对节点 $u$ 的预测重要，但对另一个节点 $v$ 不重要。基于这一动机，我们实现 $\Omega = g_\Psi(G_o, Z)$ 来解释节点 $v$ 的预测：

$$\omega_{ij} = \text{MLP}_\Psi([z_i; z_j; z_v]) \tag{10}$$

$\text{MLP}_\Psi$ is a multi-layer neural network parameterized with $\Psi$ and $[\cdot; \cdot]$ is the concatenation operation.

$\text{MLP}_\Psi$ 是以 $\Psi$ 为参数的多层神经网络，$[\cdot; \cdot]$ 是拼接操作。

Explanation network for graph classification. For graph level tasks, each graph is considered as an instance. The explanation of the prediction of a graph is not conditional to a specific node. Therefore, we specify the $\Omega = g_\Psi(G_o, Z)$ for graph classification as:

图分类的解释网络。对于图级任务，每张图被视为一个实例。图的预测解释不以特定节点为条件。因此，我们将图分类的 $\Omega = g_\Psi(G_o, Z)$ 规定为：

$$\omega_{ij} = \text{MLP}_\Psi([z_i; z_j]) \tag{11}$$

With the graph classification as an example, the architecture of PGExplainer is shown in Figure 2. The algorithms of PGExplainer for node and graph classification can be found in Appendix.

以图分类为例，PGExplainer 的架构如图 2 所示。PGExplainer 用于节点分类和图分类的算法见附录。

Computational complexity. PGExplainer is more efficient than GNNExplainer for two reasons. First, PGExplainer learns a latent variable for each edge in the original input graph with a neural network parameterized by $\Psi$, which is shared by all edges in the population of input graphs. Different from GNNExplainer, whose parameter size is linear to the number of edges, the number of parameters in PGExplainer is irrelevant to the size of the input graph, which makes PGExplainer applicable to large scale datasets. Further, since the explanation is shared among a population, a trained PGExplainer can be utilized in the inductive setting to explain new instances without retraining the explainer. To explain a new instance with $|E|$ edges in the input graph, the time complexity of PGExplainer is $O(|E|)$. As a comparison, GNNExplainer has to retrain for the new instance, leading to the time complexity of $O(T|E|)$, where $T$ is the number of epochs for retraining.

计算复杂度。PGExplainer 比 GNNExplainer 更高效，原因有二。首先，PGExplainer 用参数为 $\Psi$ 的神经网络为原始输入图中的每条边学习一个潜在变量，该网络在输入图群体中的所有边之间共享。与参数规模随边数线性增长的 GNNExplainer 不同，PGExplainer 的参数数量与输入图的大小无关，这使得 PGExplainer 适用于大规模数据集。其次，由于解释在群体间共享，训练好的 PGExplainer 可以在归纳式场景中解释新实例而无需重新训练解释器。要解释一个输入图含 $|E|$ 条边的新实例，PGExplainer 的时间复杂度为 $O(|E|)$。作为对比，GNNExplainer 必须为新实例重新训练，时间复杂度为 $O(T|E|)$，其中 $T$ 是重新训练的轮数。

4.4Regularization正则化

The framework of PGExplainer is flexible with various regularization terms to preserve desired properties on the explanation. We now discuss the regularization terms as well as their principles.

Size and entropy constraints. Following [53], to obtain compact and succinct explanations, we impose a constraint on the explanation size by adding $\|\Omega\|_1$, the $l_1$ norm on latent variables $\Omega$, as a regularization term. Besides, element-wise entropy is also be applied to further achieve discrete edge weights [53].

PGExplainer 框架灵活，可引入各种正则化项以保留解释的期望性质。下面我们讨论这些正则化项及其原理。

大小与熵约束。参照 [53]，为获得紧凑简洁的解释，我们通过加入 $\|\Omega\|_1$（潜在变量 $\Omega$ 的 $l_1$ 范数）作为正则化项来约束解释大小。此外，还应用逐元素熵以进一步实现离散的边权重 [53]。

Next, we provide more regularization terms that are compatible with PGExplainer. Note that for a fair comparison, the following regularization terms are not utilized in experimental studies in Section 5.

Budget constraint. To obtain a compact explanation, the $l_1$ norm on latent variables $\Omega$ is introduced, which penalizes all edge weights to sparsify the explanatory graph. In cases that a predefined budget $B$ is available, for example, $|G_s| \le B$, we could modify the size constraint to budget constraint:

接下来，我们提供更多与 PGExplainer 兼容的正则化项。注意，为公平比较，以下正则化项未在第 5 节的实验研究中使用。

预算约束。为获得紧凑的解释，引入潜在变量 $\Omega$ 的 $l_1$ 范数，惩罚所有边权重以使解释图稀疏。在存在预定义预算 $B$（例如 $|G_s| \le B$）的情况下，我们可以将大小约束修改为预算约束：

$$R_b = \text{ReLU}\!\left(\sum_{(i,j) \in E} \hat{e}_{ij} - B\right) \tag{12}$$

When the size of the explanatory graph is smaller than the budget $B$, the budget regularization $R_b = 0$. On the other hand, it works similarly to the size constraint when out of budget.

当解释图的大小小于预算 $B$ 时，预算正则化 $R_b = 0$；超出预算时，其作用与大小约束类似。

Connectivity constraint. In many real-life scenarios, determinant motifs are expected to be connected. Although it is claimed that GNNExplainer empirically tends to detect a small connected subgraph, the explicit constraints are not provided [53]. We propose to implement the connected constraint with the cross-entropy of adjacent edges, which connect to the same node. For instance, $(i, j)$ and $(i, k)$ both connected to the node $i$. The motivation is that is $(i, j)$ is selected in the explanatory graph, then its adjacent edge $(i, k)$ should also be included. Formally, we design the connectivity constraint as:

连通性约束。在许多真实场景中，决定性基元（motif）应保持连通。尽管有文献声称 GNNExplainer 经验上倾向于检测小的连通子图，但它并未提供显式约束 [53]。我们提出用相邻边（即连接到同一节点的边）的交叉熵来实现连通约束。例如，$(i, j)$ 和 $(i, k)$ 都连接到节点 $i$。其动机是：如果 $(i, j)$ 被选入解释图，那么其相邻边 $(i, k)$ 也应被包含。形式上，我们将连通性约束设计为：

$$H(\hat{e}_{ij}, \hat{e}_{ik}) = -\left[(1 - \hat{e}_{ij}) \log(1 - \hat{e}_{ik}) + \hat{e}_{ij} \log \hat{e}_{ik}\right] \tag{13}$$

5.1Datasets数据集

We follow the setting in GNNExplainer and construct four kinds of node classification datasets, BA-Shapes, BA-Community, Tree-Cycles, and Tree-Grids [53]. Furthermore, we also construct a graph classification datasets, BA-2motifs. Illustration of synthetic datasets is shown in Table 2. (1) BA-Shapes is a single graph consisting of a base Barabasi-Albert (BA) graph with 300 nodes and 80 “house”-structured motifs. These motifs are attached to randomly selected nodes from the BA graph. After that, random edges are added to perturb the graph. Nodes features are not assigned in BA-Shapes. Nodes in the base graph are labeled with 0; the ones locating at the top/middle/bottom of the “house” are labeled with 1,2,3, respectively. (2) BA-Community dataset consists of two BA-Shapes graphs. Two Gaussian distributions are utilized to sample node features, one for each BA-Shapes graph. Nodes are labeled based on their structural roles and community memberships, leading to 8 classes in total. (3) In the Tree-Cycles dataset, an 8-level balanced binary tree is adopted as the base graph. A set of 80 six-node cycle motifs are attached to randomly selected nodes from the base graph. (4) Tree-Grid is constructed in the same way as Tree-Cycles, except that 3-by-3 grid motifs are used to replace the cycle motifs. (5) For graph classification, we build the BA-2motifs dataset of 800 graphs. We adopt the BA graphs as base graphs. Half graphs are attached with “house” motifs and the rest are attached with five-node cycle motifs. Graphs are assigned to one of 2 classes according to the type of attached motifs.

我们遵循 GNNExplainer 的设置，构建了四类节点分类数据集：BA-Shapes、BA-Community、Tree-Cycles 和 Tree-Grids [53]。此外，我们还构建了一个图分类数据集 BA-2motifs。合成数据集的示意图见表 2。（1）BA-Shapes 是一个由包含 300 个节点的 Barabasi-Albert（BA）基图和 80 个“房子”结构基元组成的单图。这些基元被连接到从 BA 图中随机选取的节点上，之后添加随机边以扰动图。BA-Shapes 中没有分配节点特征：基图中的节点标记为 0；位于“房子”顶部/中部/底部的节点分别标记为 1、2、3。（2）BA-Community 数据集由两个 BA-Shapes 图组成，利用两个高斯分布采样节点特征，每个 BA-Shapes 图对应一个。节点根据其结构角色和社区成员身份进行标记，总共产生 8 个类别。（3）在 Tree-Cycles 数据集中，采用 8 层平衡二叉树作为基图，将一组 80 个六节点环基元连接到从基图随机选取的节点上。（4）Tree-Grid 的构建方式与 Tree-Cycles 相同，只是用 3×3 网格基元替换环基元。（5）对于图分类，我们构建了包含 800 张图的 BA-2motifs 数据集。我们采用 BA 图作为基图：一半图连接“房子”基元，其余连接五节点环基元。根据所连接基元的类型，图被分配到 2 个类别之一。

We also include a real-life dataset, MUTAG, for graph classification, which is also used in previous work [53]. It consists of 4,337 molecule graphs. Each graph is assigned to one of 2 classes based on its mutagenic effect [53, 40]. As discussed in [53, 9], carbon rings with chemical groups $\text{NH}_2$ or $\text{NO}_2$ are known to be mutagenic. We observe that carbon rings exist in both mutagen and nonmutagenic graphs, which are not discriminative. Thus, we can treat carbon rings as the shared base graphs and $\text{NH}_2$, $\text{NO}_2$ as motifs for the mutagen graphs. There are no explicit motifs for nonmutagenic ones. 5.2Baselines and experimental setup基线与实验设置

Baselines. We compare with the following baseline methods, GNNExplainer [53], a gradient-based method (GRAD) [53], graph attention network (ATT) [45], and Gradient [38]. (1) GNNExplainer is a post-hoc method providing explanations for every single instance. (2) GRAD learns weights of edges by computing gradients of GNN’s objective function w.r.t. the adjacency matrix. (3) ATT utilizes self-attention layers to distinguish edge attention weights in the input graph. Each edge’s importance is obtained by averaging its attention weights across all attention layers. (4) We first adopt Gradient in [38] to calculate the importance of each node, then calculate the importance of an edge by average the connected nodes’ importance scores.

基线方法。我们与以下基线方法进行比较：GNNExplainer [53]、基于梯度的方法（GRAD）[53]、图注意力网络（ATT）[45] 和 Gradient [38]。（1）GNNExplainer 是一种为每个实例提供解释的事后（post-hoc）方法。（2）GRAD 通过计算 GNN 目标函数对邻接矩阵的梯度来学习边的权重。（3）ATT 利用自注意力层来区分输入图中的边注意力权重，每条边的重要性通过对其在所有注意力层上的注意力权重取平均得到。（4）我们首先采用 [38] 中的 Gradient 计算每个节点的重要性，然后通过平均相连节点的重要性得分来计算边的重要性。

Experimental setup. We follow the experimental settings in GNNExplainer [53]. Specifically, for post-hoc methods including ATT, GNNExplainer, and PGExplainer, we first train a three-layer GNN and then apply these methods to explain predictions made by the GNN. Since weights in attention layers are jointly optimized with the GNN model in ATT, we thus train another GNN model with self-attention layers. We follow [1] to tune temperature $\tau$. We refer readers to the Appendix for more training details.

实验设置。我们遵循 GNNExplainer [53] 的实验设置。具体而言，对于包括 ATT、GNNExplainer 和 PGExplainer 在内的事后方法，我们首先训练一个三层 GNN，然后应用这些方法解释该 GNN 做出的预测。由于 ATT 中注意力层的权重与 GNN 模型联合优化，我们另外训练一个带自注意力层的 GNN 模型。我们参照 [1] 调节温度 $\tau$。更多训练细节请参阅附录。

6

Conclusion结论

We present PGExplainer, a parameterized method to provide a global understanding of any GNN models on arbitrary machine learning tasks by collectively explaining multiple instances. We show that PGExplainer can leverage the representations produced by GNNs to learn the underlying subgraphs that are important to the predictions made by GNNs. Furthermore, PGExplainer is more efficient due to its capacity to explain GNNs in the inductive settings, which makes PGExplainer more practical in real-life applications.

我们提出了 PGExplainer，一种参数化方法，通过集体解释多个实例，为任意机器学习任务上的任何 GNN 模型提供全局理解。我们表明，PGExplainer 能够利用 GNN 产生的表示来学习对 GNN 预测重要的底层子图。此外，由于 PGExplainer 能够在归纳式场景中解释 GNN，它更加高效，这使得 PGExplainer 在实际应用中更实用。

Broader impact更广泛影响

Graph neural networks are powerful tools that have been applied in various real-world applications, including community detection, recommendation systems, computer vision, and natural language processing [3, 13, 30, 51]. Our work can not only provide interpretable explanations with local fidelity for predictions made by GNN models, but also improve the global understanding of the model.

There are several broader impacts of using our method to explain predictions made by GNNs. First, our method can increase the transparency of applying GNNs for decision-critical applications, such as drug discovery and diagnosis. As a result, our method can help alleviate safety, and fairness risks. For example, as we show in our experiments, we could correctly identify motifs that have determinant effects on the mutagenicity of molecules. On the other hand, our method also puts GNN models at a high risk of being attacked. Our method extracts subgraphs that are important to GNNs’ behaviors. Disturbing these parts leads to significant changes in GNNs’ predictions. Besides, increasing the interpretability of GNNs may cause automation bias, such as an undue trust on GNN models.

图神经网络是强大的工具，已应用于各种真实世界场景，包括社区检测、推荐系统、计算机视觉和自然语言处理 [3, 13, 30, 51]。我们的工作不仅能为 GNN 模型的预测提供具有局部保真度的可解释解释，还能改善对模型的全局理解。

使用我们的方法解释 GNN 预测还有若干更广泛的影响。首先，我们的方法可以提高 GNN 在关键决策应用（如药物发现与诊断）中的透明度，从而有助于缓解安全与公平风险。例如，如我们实验所示，我们能够正确识别对分子致突变性有决定性影响的基元。另一方面，我们的方法也使 GNN 模型面临被攻击的高风险：我们的方法提取对 GNN 行为重要的子图，扰动这些部分会导致 GNN 预测发生显著变化。此外，提高 GNN 的可解释性可能引发自动化偏差（automation bias），例如对 GNN 模型的过度信任。
